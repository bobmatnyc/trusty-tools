Added
- The Architect's Intent Ledger (`datg.py`, seeded by `tm fleet init`) now has the Task-ID gate from the Architect prototype (#11101). A dispatch carries `Task: <id>@MMDD` for an open task. A missing, unknown or reused id warns and is logged to `inbox/task-compliance.jsonl`. With `config.task_mode` set to `refuse`, it follows the dispatch mode instead. The G2 work-card check and the SessionStart rules name the card with `Task:` instead of `Card:`, and the hand-back ends with the `Task: <id>@MMDD | done: … | pct: <n>` line.
- `add T` stamps `created` and warns once a ledger has more than `config.wip_cap` active tasks (default 8). In refuse mode it raises instead. Tasks may be `parked`, and parked tasks do not count toward the cap. A new `pulse` op records a task's pulse note, and `--state-dir` / `$DATG_STATE_DIR` pin the exact state directory. The digest shows `owed (n/8)`.
- New seeded scripts: `datg_task.py`, `task_tag.py` (the tag grammar, including `parse_pulse`) and `task_pulse.py` with its helper module `task_pulse_io.py` (applies hand-back tags as reported figures; not wired into the poller). The Rust `tm dbatg` writer does not stamp `created`, accept `parked` or apply `pulse` yet.
