Changed
- The Architect skills `tm-fleet-check` and `tm-context-refresh` and the Architect `CLAUDE.md` template are read from content (`instructions/architect/skills/` and `instructions/architect/templates/CLAUDE.md`) at runtime instead of being compiled into `tm`. `tm fleet init` reads all three before it writes anything and refuses, naming the missing or empty file, when the content lacks one. An Architect launch and `tm fleet status` report the same error and deploy no skill. A content release older than this change does not carry the files: run `tm content update` (#9132).
