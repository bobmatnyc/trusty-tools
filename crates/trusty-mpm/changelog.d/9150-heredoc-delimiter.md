Fixed
- `tm hook --pm-guard` refuses a Bash command whose here-document delimiter word quotes or escapes a space, tab, `<`, `>`, `|`, `;`, `&`, `(` or `)` (`<<'A B'`, `<<"A;B"`, `<<A\ B`), or leaves a quote or trailing `\` open on the `<<` line. The refusal holds under every bypass. Before, the guard cut such a word at the break byte and ended the body at a later line than bash and zsh do, so `cat <<'A B'` … `A B` / `echo "$(rm -rf /)"` / `A` read the `$(rm -rf /)` as quoted body text and allowed it past the destructive-delete floor. A plain delimiter such as `<<'EOF'` is unaffected (#9150).
