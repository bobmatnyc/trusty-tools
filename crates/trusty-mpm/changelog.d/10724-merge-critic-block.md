Fixed
- `tm pr merge` refuses while a code-critic BLOCK is pinned to the PR's head commit and no later APPROVE or WARN is pinned to it. The refusal names the review or comment and the sha. A verdict is the line `## Verdict: <APPROVE|WARN|BLOCK>`; a review is pinned to the commit it was submitted on, and `, pinned to <sha>` on the line overrides that. A BLOCK that names no commit refuses until a later APPROVE or WARN is pinned to the head. A BLOCK on an older head only warns that no verdict covers the head. If the comments and reviews cannot be read, the merge is refused. A raw `gh pr merge` still bypasses this check.
