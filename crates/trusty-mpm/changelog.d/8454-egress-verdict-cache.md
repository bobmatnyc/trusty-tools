Added
- The daemon answers `POST /api/v1/channels/egress-verdict` with the channel route policy's readiness verdict from its policy snapshot, and `tm hook --egress-guard` asks it first for a channel-tainted session's path tool call instead of loading the policy inline (~1.5 s). The daemon serves a verdict only for its own home, from a snapshot under 60 s old, with a nonzero policy fingerprint, and judges the verdict on the report the snapshot publishes at that query; a denied policy is answered as not ready. In every other case, and when the daemon is down or does not answer within 300 ms, the hook loads the policy inline as before. A non-2xx status or a malformed reply denies the call as `egress-policy-not-ready`. No answer allows a call the inline check would deny (#8454, #8433).
