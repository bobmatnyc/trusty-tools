Changed
- An owner's reply to an `ask_owner` question with options resolves it only when the reply equals one option's label or its number from 1 (case and outer whitespace ignored). The asker's pane then gets `[channel answer Q-<id>: option <n> "<label>"]`, built from the stored question with no byte of the reply, so no taint is written; the delivery trace still records `pending` then `delivered` (#10127).
- Free-text replies are now held rather than delivered: a reply that matches no option, and every reply to a question without options, is not resolved and taints nothing. The question stays open, an `unparsed` record (digest and length only) is appended, the audit log records `option_unmatched` or `free_text_held`, and the asker's pane gets a notice that carries no reply text. A notice that cannot be sent is settled `failed` (`notice_send_failed`) in the delivery trace for `tm doctor` and is not retried. The sandbox inbound route answers a held reply with 202 (#10127).
- Delivery-trace lines for a held-reply notice carry `"kind":"notice"` and never settle or stand in for an answer: `tm doctor` judges each question's answer from answer lines only (a line without `kind` is one), and lists a failed or unsettled notice separately, saying the question is still open (#10127).
