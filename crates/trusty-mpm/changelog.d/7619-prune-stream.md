Fixed
- `tm session prune-worktrees` prints a line for each worktree the pass inspects or removes, with or without `--merged-prs`, and a keepalive line every 15s while the daemon is busy. It reads the new socket stream `mpm.managed.prune_worktrees_stream` and falls back to the unary method, with a stderr note, on a daemon that does not serve it. Ctrl-C, or a killed CLI, stops the pass before its next worktree; a removal in flight finishes. A Ctrl-C at any point of the command is seen: one between the preview and `--force` sends no removal, and one during the unary fallback ends the command. The `--force` banner states this before the removal starts. `--force --detach` lets the daemon finish the removal after the command exits. The CLI fails when the daemon sends nothing for 120s, or when the stream ends without its result (#7619).
