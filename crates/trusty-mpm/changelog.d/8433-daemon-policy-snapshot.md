Added
- The daemon now holds the chat-channel route policy behind a snapshot that refreshes at most every 5 s. Its egress and inbound checks deny everything when the last good refresh is older than 60 s, when the load is denied (a host-ceiling fault or overlapping routes), or when no load has completed. A refresh that panics, or that keeps a project's last-good routes for any reason other than a verdict on the reviewed content (an unreviewed edit, a branch switch, a malformed file), counts toward the 60 s limit, and the age is measured after the refresh returns. A route edit that is not committed on the default branch of a project listed in `config.yaml` has no effect. Nothing calls the snapshot yet; it is built with the `channels` feature only (Refs #8433, #8454).
