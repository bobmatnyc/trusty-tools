Fixed
- An answer to a managed session's pending decision (`POST /api/v1/sessions/managed/{id}/answer`, `mpm.managed.answer`) now takes the decision once, under the session store's lock, before it types into the pane or launches the runtime. Two concurrent answers reach the pane once, and two concurrent answers to a front-gate decision launch the runtime once. A repeat answer gets a 409 that names the first answerer and the time (`decision <id> already answered by <X> at <T>`), with the same text over the socket. A send or launch that fails after the claim returns a 500 and the decision stays closed, so a half-typed answer is never sent twice. A claim that cannot be written to `sessions.json` sends nothing and leaves the decision pending. A write that read the session before a claim, such as a `tm session send` in flight, can no longer put the answered decision back; every session-record write keeps a newer decision, except a decommission, which clears it from the decommissioned record. The guarantee holds within one daemon process. A write from another process (the supervisor) that lands between its own reload of `sessions.json` and its rename can still undo a claim (Refs #9841).
