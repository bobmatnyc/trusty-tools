Changed
- `tm tui` now opens on a console home screen: one tile per trusty-* service the console knows (search, memory, analyze, review, mpm, agents, events, console), each showing up, degraded, down, not installed, timeout or on demand from the service's existing socket health method, socket or `http_addr` file. Probes run on background tasks with a 3 s deadline, so a slow service never blocks the keyboard. Enter on the MPM tile opens the projects dashboard as before, and `q` there returns to the home screen. Enter on another tile opens its service view: health detail plus the service's `console_metrics` report over its stdio MCP command, fetched in the background with a 10 s deadline, cached for 30 s, and shown as "unavailable" when the service is not running or the call fails. Esc returns home. `tm projects`, `tm attach` and `tm tui --single-pane` are unchanged (#10491).
