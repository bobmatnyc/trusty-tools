Changed
- The `tm doctor` `events` row now tells a hung trusty-events bus from a serving one (#10071). After the socket accepts, it opens one subscription asking for a heartbeat every 500 ms and waits up to 2 s for the first frame, then closes the connection. A frame is `ok`. Silence is `fail` (hung), with the remedy `tctl restart trusty-events` and the log `~/.trusty-events/logs/stderr.log`. A bus too old to accept `heartbeat_ms` is `warn`, naming the version skew; tm and trusty-events ship in lockstep with no fallback. The `ok` detail no longer says that liveness is not checked.
