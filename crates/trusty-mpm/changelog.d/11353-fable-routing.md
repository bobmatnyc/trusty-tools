Added
- Model resolution recognizes the Fable tier (`fable` alias, `[models.tiers] fable`, default `claude-fable-5-2`) and reserves it for PM sessions and engineer and research roles. Any other agent that asks for Fable, through `--model`, `[models.agents]`, frontmatter or `[models] default`, runs on Opus, and the substitution is logged with the agent, requested and resolved models. See #11353.
- `tm hook --pm-guard` refuses a PM `Agent`/`Task` dispatch that would run any role other than PM, engineer (any `*-engineer`, including `data-engineer`) or research on a Fable model — whether the model comes from `CLAUDE_CODE_SUBAGENT_MODEL`, the dispatch's `model` argument, the deployed agent's frontmatter, or an `inherit` from a Fable PM session. New guard policy GP-051 `fable-cap`. See #11353.
