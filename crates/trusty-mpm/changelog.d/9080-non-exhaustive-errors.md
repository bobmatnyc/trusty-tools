Breaking
- Eight public error enums are now `#[non_exhaustive]`, so a later variant is not a semver break: `session_manager::StoreError` (`session_manager::store::StoreError`), `session_manager::RecordError` (`session_manager::record::RecordError`), `session_manager::store_integrity::RepairError`, `deliverable::StoreError` (`deliverable::store::StoreError`), `runtime::RuntimeError`, `core::session_mcp_scope::ScopeError`, `core::gh_identity::GhIdentityError` and `daemon::managed_routes::inproject_cold_start::ColdStartError`. A `match` on one of them outside trusty-mpm needs a `_` arm; building a variant, `?` propagation and the `Display` messages are unchanged ([#9080](https://github.com/bobmatnyc/trusty-tools/issues/9080))
