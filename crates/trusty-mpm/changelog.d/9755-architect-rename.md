- The fleet role is named Architect throughout tm's code (#9755). The public Rust names move from `supervisor` to `architect` with no re-export shims: the modules `session_manager::{architect, architect_floor, architect_register}`, `daemon::managed_routes::architect` and `client::http_client::architect`, and items such as `SessionProfile::Architect`, `SessionKind::{Architect, ArchitectAux}`, `ArchitectFloor`, `ArchitectRegistration`, `ArchitectConfig` and `MpmConfig.architect`. The full list is in `scripts/semver-accepted-breaks/trusty-mpm-1.9.0.txt`.
- tm accepts both the legacy and the Architect names for one release: `profile = "supervisor"` or `"architect"` in `.trusty-mpm.toml`, the `[supervisor]` or `[architect]` table in `~/.trusty-mpm/config.toml`, the `TRUSTY_MPM_SESSION_PROFILE` stamp `supervisor` or `architect`, the stored session kinds `supervisor`/`supervisor_aux` or `architect`/`architect_aux`, the register route `POST /api/v1/sessions/managed/supervisor` or `.../managed/architect`, the RPC method `mpm.managed.register_supervisor` or `mpm.managed.register_architect`, and the output style `trusty-mpm-supervisor` or `trusty-mpm-architect`.
- Writers still emit the legacy values in this release, so an older tm keeps reading what this one writes: the `supervisor` profile, stamp and session kinds, the `[supervisor]` table unless `[architect]` already exists, the `trusty-mpm-supervisor` output style, and the legacy register route from the client.
- When `~/.trusty-mpm/config.toml` has both an `[architect]` and a `[supervisor]` table, `[architect]` wins and the two are not merged; `tm doctor` warns about the duplicate.
- The instruction files stay under `content/instructions/supervisor/`. They move to `architect/` in a follow-up content release, after this tm is released and installed; tm already loads either directory.
