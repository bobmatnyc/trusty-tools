Fixed
- An inbound channel message is no longer typed into a pane whose `claude` is suspended (Ctrl-Z) or running as a background job while the pane's shell holds the prompt. The pane write gate now requires the harness to be running and to own the pane's tty foreground process group (`ps` state, `pgid`, `tpgid`); a stopped or background harness, or a `ps` read that fails or cannot be parsed, is refused as `harness_unconfirmed` and nothing is typed. `tm fleet relaunch` and Architect helper registration keep their existence check, so a suspended `claude` is never read as absent (#10136).
