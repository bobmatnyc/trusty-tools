Breaking
- The fleet role's public Rust names move from `supervisor` to `architect` with no re-export shims (#9755), so code that names the old paths or items no longer compiles; use the Architect names below. Modules: `session_manager::{supervisor, supervisor_floor, supervisor_register}`, `daemon::managed_routes::supervisor` and `client::http_client::supervisor` become `architect`, `architect_floor`, `architect_register`, `architect` and `architect`, and the items they hold (`ProtectedVerb`, `KillVerdict`, `SidecarRole`, `RegisteredSession`, `RegistrationReport`, `RegisterError`, `BindingVerifier`, `LaunchRecordBinding`, `RegisterCallError`, `collector_session_name`, `COLLECTOR_ROLE`) move with them. Renamed: `SupervisorFloor`, `SupervisorRegistration` and `SupervisorConfig` to `ArchitectFloor`, `ArchitectRegistration` and `ArchitectConfig`; `supervisor_prompt` and `supervisor_routes` to `architect_prompt` and `architect_routes`; `register_supervisor_route` to `register_architect_route`; `SessionManager::register_supervisor` and `DaemonClient::register_supervisor` to `register_architect`; `ManagedSessionId::for_supervisor` to `for_architect`; `SessionProfile::is_supervisor` to `is_architect`; `TmuxDriver::supervisor_floor` and the `ManagedTmuxDriver` trait method of that name to `architect_floor`; `SUPERVISOR_PROFILE_ID`, `SUPERVISOR_OUTPUT_STYLE_ID`, `SUPERVISOR_MODEL`, `SUPERVISOR_SECTIONS` and `SUPERVISOR_ROLE` to `ARCHITECT_WRITTEN_PROFILE_ID`, `ARCHITECT_WRITTEN_OUTPUT_STYLE_ID`, `ARCHITECT_MODEL`, `ARCHITECT_SECTIONS` and `ARCHITECT_ROLE`. Enums: `SessionKind::{Supervisor, SupervisorAux}` and `SessionProfile::Supervisor` become `Architect`/`ArchitectAux` and `Architect`, and `TwinRefusal::NotSupervisor` becomes `NotArchitectProfile`, so an exhaustive `match` on them needs the new arms. `MpmConfig.supervisor` becomes `MpmConfig.architect`, and `MpmConfig` gains `legacy_supervisor` (the pre-rename `[supervisor]` table), so a struct literal must set both. Serialized values keep their legacy spelling, so stored files and the wire format are unchanged.
