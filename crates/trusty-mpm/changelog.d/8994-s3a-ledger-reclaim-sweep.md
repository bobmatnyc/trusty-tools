Added
- The daemon's hourly worktree-reclaim tick now also runs a ledger reclaim pass over every tree in the worktree ledger whose PR merged or whose branch is gone from `origin` with nothing unpushed. It only reports what it would remove until `disk.ledger_reclaim_remove: true` is set in the trusty-mpm config; an absent or unreadable config never removes. A tree is kept when its HEAD is detached or on another branch than the recorded one, when its branch gained commits after the PR merged, and when any lookup, fetch, dirt check, liveness check or removal fails. Removal goes through the existing pre-delete re-check and remover, a process standing in the tree keeps it, and a tree is recorded removed only after it is confirmed gone from disk (#8994).
- `tm worktrees` lists what the reclaim pass may do with each stale candidate, or why it keeps it. `tm worktrees` runs one bounded `git fetch --prune origin` per repository it asks about a branch; the new `--no-fetch` flag skips that fetch and every `gh` call, and reports the affected trees as unresolved, never as gone or merged (#8994).
