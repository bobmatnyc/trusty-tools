Added
- The Architect's Intent Ledger can record that an owner ruling replaces an earlier one (#10960). `datg.py update NEW --answer ... --source owner ... --supersedes OLD` stores `superseded_by: NEW` on the earlier ruling. The link is explicit; a later owner turn never supersedes by inference. A superseded ruling no longer counts as current for the policy-conflict check, the resume digest lists it as history with its successor, and a link whose successor is not an owner ruling in the ledger is a FLAG line.
- `relay.sh` refuses an owner-labelled relay whose quote and cite resolve to a superseded owner ruling, and names the successor and the current ruling (#10960). A successor with `until` only flags the cite. A label marked `superseded` relays the ruling as history. A ledger that cannot be read or parsed refuses the cite; a missing ledger holds no link. Unlinked rulings and text with no owner label are unchanged.
