Added
- The Architect's Intent Ledger can record that an owner ruling replaces an earlier one (#10960). `datg.py update NEW --answer ... --source owner ... --supersedes OLD` stores `superseded_by: NEW` on the earlier ruling. The link is explicit; a later owner turn never supersedes by inference. A superseded ruling no longer counts as current for the policy-conflict check, the resume digest lists it as history with its successor, and a link whose successor is not an owner ruling in the ledger is a FLAG line.
- `relay.sh` refuses an owner-labelled relay whose quote and cite resolve to a superseded owner ruling, and names the successor and the current ruling (#10960). The cite is only flagged when every successor on the chain carries an `until` that is still ahead; a chain ending in an unbounded ruling, or an `until` that has passed, refuses. A label marked `superseded` relays the ruling as history. A damaged ledger record (an unreadable or corrupt schedule, `state.json` or archive line) refuses only a cite whose link chain it may hold; every other owner relay is sent with a `FLAG DAMAGED LEDGER` line on stderr and a `finding` row in `relay-provenance.jsonl`. A missing ledger holds no link. Unlinked rulings and text with no owner label are unchanged.
