Added
- The daemon records every change to the chat-channel routes in effect, and the first load, in `~/.trusty-tools/trusty-mpm/channels/audit.jsonl` (mode 0600, directory 0700). Each line names the project, the commit the routes were verified at, the author of the last routes change at that commit as `"<name> (inferred)"` and its commit time; when git cannot say, the author is `unknown (inferred)`. A refresh that moves no route, such as an unrelated commit, writes nothing. No route name, recipient or file content is written (Refs #9832, #8433).
- A route change that cannot be recorded does not go live: the daemon keeps the routes already in effect, logs an error, and retries on the next refresh, so past the 60 s max age every channel check denies until the log is writable again. A first load that cannot be recorded leaves the policy never loaded (Refs #9832).
- `channels::audit_log::ChannelAudit` writes inbound-drop and refused-send lines to the same file, for the inbound and egress paths that follow (Refs #9832).
