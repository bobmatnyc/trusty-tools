Breaking
- Every public enum of the `trusty_memory` no-default-features API is `#[non_exhaustive]`. Nine engine enums gain the attribute: `engine::palace::RoomType`, `engine::retrieval::{ChunkCheck, ForgetOutcome}`, `engine::semantic_consolidation::ConsolidationAction` (also `engine::ConsolidationAction`), `engine::share::import::ImportOutcome` (also `engine::share::ImportOutcome` and `engine::ImportOutcome`), `engine::store::concurrent_open::OpenIntent` (also `engine::store::OpenIntent`), `engine::store::kg_redb::copy_swap::CompactStep`, and `engine::store::kg_redb::{BatchWriteOp, BatchOpResult}`. On the default `server` build, `tools::recall_rulings::DegradedReason` gains it too. A `match` on any of them outside trusty-memory needs a `_` arm; building a variant, comparing with `==`, and the serde wire shapes of `RoomType`, `ConsolidationAction` and `DegradedReason` are unchanged. New variants can then land without a further breaking change ([#9278](https://github.com/bobmatnyc/trusty-tools/issues/9278))
