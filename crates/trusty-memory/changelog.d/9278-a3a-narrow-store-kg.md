Breaking
- The knowledge-graph store modules of `trusty_memory::engine::store` expose only what a caller outside the library uses. `kg_writer` is no longer public, `kg_store` is `#[doc(hidden)]`, and in `kg`, `kg_redb` and `KnowledgeGraph` every item with no outside consumer is now `pub(crate)`: 340 of the 463 public paths in these modules leave the API (329 narrowed, 11 hidden), including the `kg_redb` root re-exports of the `copy_swap`, `snapshot` and `stats` items, `kg_redb::Drawer` and `kg_redb::Triple`. The kept surface is `KnowledgeGraph` and its 17 methods that tests or trusty-mpm call, `Triple`, `KgEdge::predicate`, the `AdjacencyDesync` error those methods document for downcasting, `KgStoreRedb` with 9 methods, `BatchWriteOp`, `BatchOpResult`, `READ_ONLY_ERROR_MSG`, and the `CompactFaultHook`, `CompactStep`, `PalaceSnapshot`, `SnapshotError`, `SnapshotManifest` and `KgRedbStats` types that other public signatures name. Every disposition is recorded in `docs/reference/trusty-memory-engine-api-audit.tsv` ([#9278](https://github.com/bobmatnyc/trusty-tools/issues/9278))
