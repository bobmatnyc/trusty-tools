Breaking
- `trusty_memory::engine::retrieval`, `engine::registry`, `engine::palace` and `engine::palace_emptiness`, with their engine-root re-exports, expose only what a caller outside the library uses: 348 of their 510 audited public paths leave the API. 261 are narrowed to `pub(crate)`, 17 that only unit tests call move behind `#[cfg(test)]`, 24 are deleted, and 46 go with the removed engine-root re-exports; none are hidden. Most of the retrieval layer functions (`recall`, `recall_scoped`, `retrieve_l2`, `recall_across_palaces*` and the rest), the alias-repair and Tier C types, `RecallScope`, the relevance floor, `CrossPalaceRecall`, `ForgetOutcome::as_str`, the rename option and outcome types, and every `PalaceRegistry` and `PalaceHandle` method and field no outside caller names are crate-only. `PalaceHandle::compact_vector_orphans` and `EmbedHealth::recorded_failures` are crate-only, so no public A4 item returns a value whose fields a caller cannot read; no field is made public again. The deleted items have no caller anywhere: the `palace::Room` and `palace::Wing` structs and their `engine::Room` / `engine::Wing` re-exports (wings and rooms are palace-store rows), the `retrieval::RetrievalLayers` stub with `L0Identity` and `L1Essential`, and `PalaceHandle::with_decay_config`. Ruling 2026-10-14 (ADR-0066 narrow-first): the chunk backfill surface (`PalaceHandle::reembed_slice`, `SliceBounds`, `SliceReport`, `SliceStop`, `ChunkMarker`, `ChunkProgress`, `ChunkState`, `CHUNK_MARKER_FILE`, `MARKER_RESCAN_AFTER`, `EmbedHealth::chunk_progress`) is crate-only until its background caller lands in #9735 PR 2. Ruling 2026-10-14: the engine-root re-exports `engine::Drawer`, `engine::DrawerType`, `engine::Palace`, `engine::PalaceId`, `engine::RoomType` and `engine::PalaceHandle` are removed, so each type has one path before 1.0; name them as `engine::palace::{Drawer, DrawerType, Palace, PalaceId, RoomType}` and `engine::retrieval::PalaceHandle`. The 162 kept paths are what trusty-mpm (`PalaceRegistry`, `PalaceHandle`, `Palace`, `PalaceId`, `RoomType`, `RecallResult`, `RememberOptions`, `VerifyNotStored`, the default-embedder recall functions), the integration tests and other public signatures name. Every disposition is recorded in `docs/reference/trusty-memory-engine-api-audit.tsv` ([#9278](https://github.com/bobmatnyc/trusty-tools/issues/9278))
- `trusty_memory::engine::palace_not_found` (`PalaceNotFound`, `ExistingPalaces`, `MAX_LISTED_PALACES`) and their engine-root re-exports are crate-only. `PalaceNotFound` is the path-free form of `engine::store::palace_store::PalaceStoreError::NotFound`, and the not-found text every surface relays is unchanged ([#9278](https://github.com/bobmatnyc/trusty-tools/issues/9278))
