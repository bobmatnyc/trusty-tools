Breaking
- `trusty_memory::engine::retrieval`, `engine::registry`, `engine::palace` and `engine::palace_emptiness`, with their engine-root re-exports, expose only what a caller outside the library uses: 243 of their 510 public paths leave the API. 202 are narrowed to `pub(crate)`, 17 that only unit tests call move behind `#[cfg(test)]`, and 24 are deleted; none are hidden. Most of the retrieval layer functions (`recall`, `recall_scoped`, `retrieve_l2`, `recall_across_palaces*` and the rest), the alias-repair and Tier C types, `RecallScope`, the relevance floor, `CrossPalaceRecall`, `ForgetOutcome::as_str`, the rename option and outcome types, and every `PalaceRegistry` and `PalaceHandle` method and field no outside caller names are crate-only. `PalaceHandle::compact_vector_orphans` and `EmbedHealth::recorded_failures` are crate-only, so no public A4 item returns a value whose fields a caller cannot read; no field is made public again. The deleted items have no caller anywhere: the `palace::Room` and `palace::Wing` structs and their `engine::Room` / `engine::Wing` re-exports (wings and rooms are palace-store rows), the `retrieval::RetrievalLayers` stub with `L0Identity` and `L1Essential`, and `PalaceHandle::with_decay_config`. The 267 kept paths are what trusty-mpm (`PalaceRegistry`, `PalaceHandle`, `Palace`, `PalaceId`, `RoomType`, `RecallResult`, `RememberOptions`, `VerifyNotStored`, the default-embedder recall functions), the integration tests and other public signatures name, plus the chunk backfill surface (`PalaceHandle::reembed_slice`, `SliceBounds`, `SliceReport`, `SliceStop`, `ChunkMarker`, `ChunkProgress`, `ChunkState`), which stays public pending a ruling. Every disposition is recorded in `docs/reference/trusty-memory-engine-api-audit.tsv` ([#9278](https://github.com/bobmatnyc/trusty-tools/issues/9278))
