Breaking
- The remaining fifteen `trusty_memory::engine` modules (`dream`, `semantic_consolidation`, `share`, `filter`, `community`, `embed`, `maintenance_log`, `maintenance_lease`, `timeouts`, `analytics`, `decay`, `git`, `content_hash`, `room_identity`, `wing_identity`) and their engine-root re-exports expose only what a caller outside the library uses: 334 of their 634 audited public paths leave the API, and nothing is deleted. 322 are narrowed to `pub(crate)` (30 of them are removed re-export names) and 12 are hidden. `engine::embed` is crate-only: name `trusty_common::embedder::{Embedder, FastEmbedder, EMBED_DIM, embed_one}`. `engine::maintenance_log` and `engine::maintenance_lease` are crate-only, with the `engine::LeaseStatus` and `engine::MaintenanceLease` re-exports. The `engine::{ConsolidationProvider, ConsolidationResult, OllamaInference, OpenRouterInference, resolve_consolidation_provider}` re-exports are removed and the items are crate-only, as are most `dream`, `semantic_consolidation`, `filter`, `timeouts`, `analytics` and `decay` items. `MockInference` (`engine::MockInference`, `engine::semantic_consolidation::MockInference`) is compiled only for the crate's tests and under the `memory-core-test-support` feature, and `share::assert_superseded_by` is `#[doc(hidden)]` (ruling D3). `store::embed_ledger`, `store::vector::AliasScan` and `store::vector::CompactionResult` are crate-only, as the A4 signatures that named them are. On the default `server` build, `commands::kuzu_import::screen::SecretRule` and `service::DreamStatusPayload::fading` are crate-only too; the `dream_status` wire shape is unchanged. The 300 kept paths are the share export/import format, the content hash and the room and wing id helpers (ADR-0067, ADR-0027), `Dreamer::{new, dream_cycle, with_after_cycle}`, `DreamConfig` with every field (until A2) and the public fields of its `FadingParams`, `SemanticConsolidationConfig` and `FilterConfig` members, so a caller can still tune it, `DreamStats::{merged, pruned}`, `AfterCycle`, `FilterConfig`, `find_communities`, `partition` and `KnowledgeGap`, the types those signatures name, and the items no non-test code calls, which stay public until the owner rules on deleting them. Every disposition is recorded in `docs/reference/trusty-memory-engine-api-audit.tsv` ([#9278](https://github.com/bobmatnyc/trusty-tools/issues/9278))
