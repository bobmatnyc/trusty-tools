Breaking
- The rest of `trusty_memory::engine::store` (every module and store-root re-export outside the knowledge-graph modules A3a covered) exposes only what a caller outside the library uses: 505 of its 663 public paths leave the API: 437 are narrowed to `pub(crate)`, none hidden, and 68 are deleted with `payload_store`. The `l1_cache`, `redb_open`, `room_backfill`, `room_plan` and `wings` modules are crate-only, and so are the store-root re-exports of their items, `ChatSession`, `ChatSessionMeta`, `OpenMode`, `ReadOnlyRedb`, `EmbedFailure`, `PalaceStoreError`, `RoomRecord`, `RoomSummary`, `VectorHit`, `DeadlinedWrite`, `WriteTxnError` and the room functions. In `chat_sessions`, `concurrent_open`, `embed_ledger`, `format_backup`, `hnsw_store`, `palace_format`, `palace_store`, `rooms`, `vector` and `write_deadline`, every item with no outside caller is `pub(crate)`. Three methods with no caller anywhere are deleted: `rooms::resolve_or_create_room`, `hnsw_store::HnswStore::is_empty` and `vector::UsearchStore::path`. The 158 kept paths are what trusty-agents (`chat_sessions::ChatMessage`), trusty-mpm (`OpenIntent`, `PalaceStore::metadata_present`, `palace_format::{MIGRATION_RECORD, PALACE_FORMAT_SUPPORTED}`), the integration tests and other public engine signatures name. Every disposition is recorded in `docs/reference/trusty-memory-engine-api-audit.tsv` ([#9278](https://github.com/bobmatnyc/trusty-tools/issues/9278))
- `trusty_memory::engine::store::payload_store` is deleted, with its store-root re-exports `PayloadStore` and `PayloadRow`. Nothing in or outside the library calls it: its only consumer was the retired open-mpm `TrustyBackedMemoryStore`, and dead API stays out of the 1.x contract. No palace open path, format check, migration or backup reads or writes its table, so existing palaces open unchanged ([#9278](https://github.com/bobmatnyc/trusty-tools/issues/9278))
