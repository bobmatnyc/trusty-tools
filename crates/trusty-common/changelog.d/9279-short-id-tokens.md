Fixed
- Recall keeps keywords of two characters, such as `e1`, `v2`, `fe` and the `42` of `#42`, in the closet index and the query. A drawer whose content holds an id-shaped query token (two characters, or three with a digit) gains 0.3 in L2 and L3, so "ruling e1" ranks the E1 ruling first. Before, tokens shorter than three bytes were dropped and the drawer ranked 12th. The boost applies only to a rare id: one held by at most max(2, candidates / 5) of the fetched candidates. A common short word such as `pm` or `pr` earns neither the id boost nor the closet boost, so ordinary queries keep their order. Deep recall (L3) fetches three times `top_k` vector candidates when the query names an id. `am`, `go`, `id`, `vs`, `hi`, `oh`, `ok`, `up`, `my`, `us` and `me` join the stop words (#9279).
- L2 and L3 scores above 0.9 are compressed below 1.0 instead of clamped to 1.0, so strong matches keep their similarity order. Scores at or below 0.9 are unchanged (#9279).
