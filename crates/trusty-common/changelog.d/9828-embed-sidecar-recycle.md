Added
- The embed sidecar supervisor recycles the sidecar gracefully when its footprint passes a ceiling (`TRUSTY_EMBEDDERD_RECYCLE_FOOTPRINT_MB`, default 4096), its age passes a maximum (`TRUSTY_EMBEDDERD_RECYCLE_MAX_AGE_SECS`, default 21600) or its embed-call count passes a maximum (`TRUSTY_EMBEDDERD_RECYCLE_MAX_CALLS`, default 100000). The replacement starts before the old sidecar is drained and stopped, so no in-flight call fails, and a recycle never counts toward `TRUSTY_EMBEDDERD_MAX_RESTARTS`. Three failed footprint reads recycle once, then the footprint trigger stays off until a read succeeds. New public API: `RecycleConfig` (the `SupervisorConfig::recycle` field), `RecycleHooks`, `RecycleStats`, `RecycleSnapshot`, `RecycleTrigger`, `process_recycle_stats` and `EmbedderSupervisor::with_recycle_hooks` (#9828).
