Added
- The chunk backfill (`PalaceHandle::reembed_slice`) now splits a stored drawer whose text needs more than 4096 chunks, instead of recording it as a failure and retrying it once a day (#9735). The head drawer keeps its id, `fact_key` and KG triples and shrinks to the first part; the other parts are new drawers with deterministic ids. Every part carries `split-of:<head id>` and `split-part:k/N` tags and the head's room, importance, type, tags, creation time and expiry. Parts hold at most 1024 chunks and embed in later slices. The original content hash is journalled under the new `DeletionReason::SplitOversized`. Forgetting the head forgets every part and journals each one. Dream dedup never merges a split part. Remember, import and rebuild still refuse such text.
