Changed
- `FastEmbedder` passes `max_length` 256 to fastembed, so every all-MiniLM-L6-v2 input truncates at 256 tokens on every host, as CI and sentence-transformers do. fastembed truncates at the smaller of that option and the cached `tokenizer_config.json` `model_max_length`. Hosts holding the old cache (`Qdrant/all-MiniLM-L6-v2-onnx` revision `5f1b8cd78bc4`, `model_max_length` 512) used to embed inputs of 257-512 tokens in full; they now truncate at 256. Vectors already stored on those hosts for inputs over 256 tokens keep their tail until the text is re-embedded, and nothing forces a re-embed (#9275).
