Breaking
- The palace format is now 2 (`PALACE_FORMAT_SUPPORTED`): it permits `{uuid}#{n}` chunk rows in the vector index. A format-1 palace is stamped 2 at its first writer-intent open, behind a verified backup; nothing is rewritten or re-embedded. Until then a read-only open refuses it with `FormatNeedsMigration` (#9275).
- A format-0 palace, and the record of a 0 -> 1 migration a 0.33.x binary left interrupted, are refused `FormatTooOld` under every open intent; open the palace with trusty-memory 0.33.x first (ADR-0067 D3 rule 2, N-1 only) (#9275).
- A format-2 palace cannot be downgraded: trusty-memory 0.33.x refuses it `FormatTooNew`. To roll back, restore the format-migration backup and delete the palace's `index.usearch.redb`, then re-embed with `palace_reembed`. Binaries older than trusty-memory 0.33.0 have no format gate and must not open a format-2 palace (#9275).
- The 1 -> 2 migration step fails closed: it commits nothing unless the KG it holds is at format 1 with triple key v1. The 0 -> 1 triple-key rewrite is removed (#9275).
