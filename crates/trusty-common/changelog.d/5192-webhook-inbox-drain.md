`webhook_relay::drain_once` reads the receiver's inbox back out and drives a `DeliveryProcessor`, removing an entry only after the pipeline accepted it. A delivery is recorded in a `<inbox>/processed/` ledger before its entry is removed and the ledger is consulted before any processor runs, so a drainer that dies between accepting a delivery and unlinking it cannot cause the work to be repeated — the `delivery_id` deduplication the relay contract requires now lives in the drain rather than in each receiver. Exclusive per-entry claims use an `flock` the kernel releases on process death, so an interrupted delivery stays claimable. Failures are counted in a durable sidecar, bounded, and quarantined under `<inbox>/quarantine/` rather than deleted; `quarantined_count` exposes that to `trusty-console` (#5192).
