`webhook_relay::drain_once` reads the receiver's inbox back out and drives a `DeliveryProcessor`, removing an entry only after the pipeline accepted it. Exclusive per-entry claims use an `flock` the kernel releases on process death, so a drainer killed mid-processing leaves the delivery claimable rather than stranded. Failures are counted in a durable sidecar, bounded, and quarantined under `<inbox>/quarantine/` rather than deleted; `quarantined_count` exposes that to `trusty-console`. `WebhookListener::with_processor` and `run_until_signal_with_processor` run the drain at startup, on every accepted delivery, and on a 30-second backstop (#5192).
