Fixed
- An index write-quarantined by a transient corpus open failure (`contention` or `open_timeout`) now recovers in the running daemon: the daemon re-attempts the open every 30 s, lifts the quarantine once the chunks read back, re-derives the stages, and re-runs a schema migration that had failed for lack of a corpus. The status text that promised the state "self-heals" is now accurate, and names the retry (#8085).
- The re-open holds the index's teardown guard and permit, so a `DELETE` waits for it and a reindex, relocate or catch-up in progress makes it skip that index. It never creates a missing corpus file, and it does not re-attach a handle that was deleted, relocated or parked meanwhile (#8085).
- A re-opened corpus that cannot be read back stays quarantined as `unclassified` and keeps its refused-write count. Retries of a held lock log at DEBUG (#8085).
