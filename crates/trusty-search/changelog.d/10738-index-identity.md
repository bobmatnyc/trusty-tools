Added
- `search.index.status` reports `identity: {instance_id, generation}` (#10738). `instance_id` is 128 random bits, kept across restarts, and replaced when the index is deleted and re-created. `generation` starts at 1 and rises after a force reindex, a reindex that resets the hash cache, a migration fault, a corpus that fails to open, or a corpus created empty in place of the stored one (an old format moved aside, a deleted or pruned data dir). A failed bump or retire makes replies carry `identity: null` and `identity_error` until a later one succeeds; the old value is never reported again in that process. Both persist in `<data_dir>/index-identity/<id>.json`.
- The `search.index.file.put` reply adds `content_sha256` (SHA-256 of the indexed content) and `identity` (#10738). The hash is forgotten, the content written and the new hash recorded under the path's write lock, so concurrent puts to one path leave the hash of the content whose chunks are committed.
- New socket method `search.index.file.status` takes `{index_id, body: {path}}` and returns `{path, present, content_sha256, chunk_count, identity}` (#10738). An unknown path answers `present: false` with a null hash; an unknown index answers not-found.
