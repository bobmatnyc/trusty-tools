- `trusty-search service restart` (macOS) restarts the launchd-supervised daemon and verifies the result. It records the daemons serving the unit's data dir (`--data-dir` or `TRUSTY_DATA_DIR` in the installed plist, else the platform default), boots the unit out, terminates any of them that survived the bootout (a daemon detached from launchd, PPID 1), refuses to bootstrap while one is still alive, and then requires `/health` on the unit's own socket to report this binary's version with no old daemon left. Daemons on other data dirs are never signalled, and each signal goes only to a PID a fresh process scan still finds serving that data dir. A plist with no `ProgramArguments` (binary or malformed), or with a `--data-dir` that has no value, aborts the restart before anything is signalled instead of falling back to the platform default (#8686).
