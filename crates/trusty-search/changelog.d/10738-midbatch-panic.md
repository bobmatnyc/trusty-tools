Fixed
- A staged reindex that panics or is cancelled mid-run, before it resolves its staging corpus, is now rolled back to the live corpus (#10738). Before, the staging corpus stayed attached: puts kept answering `indexed: true` under the old generation and were discarded at the next restart. Now writes are refused from the moment the run dies until the live corpus is re-attached, the staged HNSW snapshot is discarded, and the generation rises. When the live corpus cannot be reopened, the index is quarantined, as for any failed rollback. Retry a refused put after the rollback.
