Added

- The daemon now deletes a duplicate or unregistered index, corpus included, once it has been idle for 30 days. Duplicate means a worktree root, so a worktree session's own index is covered, or an index parked as `worktree` or `operator`; unregistered means parked as `decommissioned`. Idle time runs from the latest of `last_queried_unix` and `last_indexed_unix` in `indexes.toml`, or from the park time when that is later. An index with neither stamp, a main checkout or other registered non-duplicate index, an index named in tm's active set, and a loaded, warm-pinned or reindexing index are kept. A missing root stays with the orphan reaper and its 7-day grace. Nothing is deleted when the active set is not trusted or when `indexes.toml` or `project-map.toml` cannot be read; the error is logged. The pass runs on the orphan-reaper ticker, so `TRUSTY_ORPHAN_REAP_SECS=0` turns it off too (Refs #10534).
