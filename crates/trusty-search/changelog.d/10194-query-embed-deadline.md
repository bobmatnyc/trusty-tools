- A per-index query whose embed misses `TRUSTY_QUERY_EMBED_DEADLINE_MS` (default 5000 ms) while the embedder sidecar is cold now answers from the lexical lane with `meta.vector_unavailable: true` and an `embedder_error` naming the deadline, instead of waiting out the spawn and answering `408 query_timeout`. A query pinned to `stage: semantic` answers `503 vector_unavailable`. The embed runs on a detached task, so the sidecar spawn finishes and later queries are hybrid; concurrent cold queries share one spawn, and while an abandoned embed runs later cache misses on that index degrade at once rather than queue behind it. `0` turns the deadline off (#10194).
