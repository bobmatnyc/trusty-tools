Security
- An index restored with an exclude glob that does not parse is now held instead of indexing the paths that glob was meant to exclude, such as secrets. A held index indexes nothing: watcher saves defer, `index-file` and `POST /indexes/{id}/reindex` answer `409 index_held` naming the glob, and the dropped-event rescan and boot reconcile refuse. Existing chunks stay and search keeps serving them. `GET /indexes/{id}/status` reports `status: "held"` with the reason in `last_walk_error`, and `search_health` reports `index_held`. A `PATCH /indexes/{id}/config` with valid globs lifts the hold without a restart (#9059).
