- `trusty-architect-replay record --clock SECS` freezes a recorded run's wall clock at that epoch second, so time-stamped output (a soak log, say) replays without a diff. A Python script runs unmodified under a wrapper that pins `time.time`, `time.time_ns`, the defaults of `time.localtime`, `gmtime`, `ctime`, `asctime` and `strftime`, and `datetime.now` and `utcnow`; `TZ=UTC` joins the recorded env unless `--env TZ=...` names a zone. The recording keeps the instant in a `clock` field, and replay hands it to the target in `TRUSTY_REPLAY_CLOCK`, which only a replay entry point reads, never a shipped binary. A recording with a clock is format version 3; a recording without one is unchanged (Refs [#12028](https://github.com/bobmatnyc/trusty-tools/issues/12028))
