Added
- `page_click`, `page_hover`, `page_type`, `page_press`, `page_select` and `page_scroll` act inside cross-origin child frames, addressed by the frame-scoped refs (`<frame>:<epoch>.<n>`) that `page_read` and `page_find` return. Tool names and inputs are unchanged. The action runs only in the child document the read recorded, under the lease's current top document, and only while the extension holds host permission for the child's own origin; otherwise it answers `DocumentChanged`, `StaleElement` or `PermissionNeeded` (with the child's `origin`) and never falls back to the top frame. A click stays trusted CDP input, lifted to top-frame coordinates. The reply adds `frame` (`id`, `origin`) and the `operation` audit line lists the child's host in `frames` ([#11808](https://github.com/bobmatnyc/trusty-tools/issues/11808)).
