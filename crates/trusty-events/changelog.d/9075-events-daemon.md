Added
- New crate and `trusty-events serve` daemon: the event bus, moved out of trusty-console (ADR-0065). One Unix socket, `<data dir>/trusty-events/trusty-events.sock`, takes producers' newline-delimited `HarnessEvent` frames and serves `events.subscribe { since_seq, filters }`; the first line of a connection decides which. The bus assigns one `seq`, keeps a ring (default 8192) and a day-rotated NDJSON log under `<data dir>/trusty-events/event_log/`, so `seq` and replay survive a restart (#9075).
- `events.subscribe` replays from the log and the ring after `since_seq`, then streams live events. Every event a subscriber misses (retention, a dropped log write, ring eviction, a slow reader) is reported as a `Gap { skipped }`; filtered-out events are not gaps. A `since_seq` past the bus's last `seq`, a non-zero `since_seq` without `since_id` or whose `since_id` is not the event the bus holds at that `seq` (as after a crash reassigned it), and a 65th subscriber are refused with an error frame (#9075).
- The daemon exits 1 when another instance serves the socket, the socket cannot be bound, the log cannot be opened or written, or accept fails 64 times in a row. On SIGTERM it stops assigning `seq`, lets the log writer drain, removes its socket and exits 0; a writer that does not drain within 5 s is exit 1 (#9075).
- Retention never deletes the newest day file that holds an event, so `seq` continues after more than `--retain-days` idle days (#9075).
