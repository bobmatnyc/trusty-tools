Fixed
- Ctrl-C on `trusty-okf import run` now cancels an import through an adapter (`--from adapter:<kind>`), with or without `--worker` (#10746, S9+S10 merge): the adapter request in flight fails `cancelled`, the adapter's whole process group is killed and reaped, later items are skipped `cancelled`, and the report is printed with exit 3. A cancel during the listing ends it as incomplete, so nothing is tombstoned or written. The CLI has one SIGINT waiter per process, and the worker and the adapter share its flag (`ImportSpec::cancel`, `RunCancel`). `ImportReport::cancelled` marks a cancelled run. `nix` is no longer behind the `extract-worker` feature.
