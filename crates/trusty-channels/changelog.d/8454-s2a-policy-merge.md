Added

- `policy` gains the two-file route model, still without file I/O: `parse_host` strictly parses the `channels:` section of trusty-mpm's `config.yaml` (unknown keys denied at every level; per-channel `enabled`, `projects`, optional `kinds` ceiling and connection refs; a Slack or Telegram `credential_ref` must name a bot or app token), `parse_project_file` parses a project's `routes.toml` v1 (today's gchat schema, unchanged) or v2 (adds Slack and Telegram routes and per-route rate limits), and `merge` returns a `LoadReport` that always carries a policy, empty on a deny. A host-ceiling fault or a route overlap across two files denies every route; a broken or widening project file (a kind outside the ceiling, a rate limit looser than the host default, a gchat connection that differs from the host's) refuses only that file; a disabled channel or an unlisted project drops its routes with a named finding. gchat behaviour is unchanged (refs [#8454](https://github.com/bobmatnyc/trusty-tools/issues/8454))
