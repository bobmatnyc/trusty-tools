Added
- MCP `review_pr` and `review_diff` accept an optional `symbol_context` boolean, and `run` takes `--symbol-context`. For each function or method the diff declares or edits, the reviewer sees its callers, callees and tests from the trusty-search call graph, as `## Changed symbols: callers, callees and tests`, fenced as data under a note that the graph is the indexed checkout's and may not be at the PR head. Off by default; a review without it is unchanged. It works on a local diff, against the configured index. A mistyped MCP value is an invalid-params error (#9196).
- Caps: at most 12 symbols, 6 lines per callers, callees or tests list, 2,500 characters per symbol and 24,000 in total, each cut marked. Every symbol left out is named under `Not shown:` with a fixed reason (`over symbol cap`, `over section cap`, `ambiguous`, `wrong file`, `not in the index`, `read failed`, `not reviewed`, `deadline`). An ambiguous name is left out, never resolved to the daemon's pick (#9196).
- The text is context, not evidence: it is never citable and the verifier never sees it. A search outage, a symbol graph that is not ready, or a 503 from trusty-search leaves the section out and the review running (#9196).
- The source ledger gains a `symbol_context` row after `analyze` (ten rows), with one item per changed symbol; `external_sources` moves from position 9 to 10 (#9196).
- `SearchClient` gains `call_chain`, a default-bodied method that answers `Unavailable`; `HttpSearchClient` reads it over the socket (`search.call_chain`) and over HTTP (`GET /indexes/{id}/call_chain`). Existing implementors compile unchanged (#9196).
