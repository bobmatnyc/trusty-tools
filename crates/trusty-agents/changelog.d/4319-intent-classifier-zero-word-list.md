Fixed

- **Intent classifier no longer sends short factual questions to the heavy subprocess pipeline on word count alone** ([#4319](https://github.com/bobmatnyc/trusty-tools/issues/4319)). `IntentClass::Implementation` is now reachable via exactly 4 unambiguous signals — a leading slash, a repo-file token, a snake_case identifier, or an error/stack-trace marker — with every action-verb and technical-context word list removed from that decision entirely. Seven rounds of code-critic review (culminating in ~29 counter-examples like "fix my gym session" and "debug the incident report from the fender bender" reaching Implementation) proved no word list combined with any verb can distinguish "the bug" (a software defect) from "a bug" (an insect); the fix is structural, not coverage. Along the way: verb-less bug reports now route to Research instead of Conversational, plain action verbs require real technical context to reach Implementation, and the `route.rs`-local run/build/tests/release exception (the separate Tm-vs-Tcode backend picker) is preserved.
  - Deliberate contract change: "fix the login bug" and "debug the auth middleware" now classify as Research, not Implementation. "fix main.rs" and slash commands are unaffected.
  - Regression coverage: an exhaustive 23x46 verb × technical-context-word sweep (1058 combinations), a 29-phrase hard-verb-polysemy set, a 14-sentence ordinary-noun set, and a 13-phrase "help me" set.
