Added
- `tctl install`, `start`, `stop`, `restart` and `status` manage trusty-events, the event bus daemon, as an optional stable-set member (#10042). `tctl` writes its LaunchAgent `com.trusty.events`: `trusty-events serve`, restarted only after a non-zero exit, at most every 10 s, with no socket or port key. `tctl status` reports it `healthy` when its Unix socket accepts a connection and `down` when the socket is missing or refuses (a stale socket); it does not yet check liveness beyond the accept (#10071). No other member requires it, so a host without trusty-events still completes `tctl install trusty-mpm`.
