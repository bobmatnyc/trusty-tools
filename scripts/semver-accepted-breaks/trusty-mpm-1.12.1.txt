reason  Accepted under the 2026-09-26 versioning ruling (a public-API break never forces a major version), per Architect D4 A (df-G01). cargo semver-checks against baseline 1.12.0 computes 11 major lints from three changes. (1) tmux_socket routing, Refs #10101: ed04279554, #10790, #10845, #10850, #10854 and #10880 add Session, RegisterSession and NameDrift .tmux_socket, add CommandResponse.send_error, add a socket parameter to the core::tmux send_line, send_command_line and send_launch_line helpers, to runtime::cli_launch::send_spec_launch, to daemon spawn_pid_capture and to DaemonState::grant_resumed_session. (2) project_ctl TUI session-pane rework, #10774 (with #10790 and #10841): the two-column MPM pane adds Pane::Sessions, the PendingAction session variants and HitMap.session_rows, replaces the details pane (session_details::render, DETAILS_KEYS, the open_details, close_details and details_session methods, TreeView.details, HitMap.details and details_focus) and reworks TreeRowKind and NodeId (Session and Anchor removed, Project.expanded removed), shifts Pane::Activity from 1 to 2, and changes panes::tree::render and row_line arity. Refs #10882.
