# Owner model

The Architect's consolidated model of the owner: how the owner runs the
business and makes decisions. The Architect maintains it from the owner's
rulings and consolidates it in a dreaming pass.

Files:

- `business.md`: business priorities, what the owner values, how the owner
  weighs goals.
- `process.md`: how the owner wants work run: delivery, review, release,
  delegation.
- `decision-making.md`: how the owner decides: defaults, when the owner
  overrides, what is delegated and what is kept.
- `risk-tolerance.md`: where the owner accepts risk and where not
  (production, security, money, data, people, external messages).
- `detail-and-communication.md`: the level of detail and the form the owner
  wants.
- `contradictions.md`: open conflicts between entries, to ask at the next
  "ask".
- `dream-log.md`: one line per consolidation pass.

Entry format, one bullet each:
`- <statement>. [stated|confirmed|inferred] Evidence: <date> <quote or ruling id>; ...`

Confidence:

- stated: the owner said it as a general rule.
- confirmed: three or more independent rulings show it, or the owner
  confirmed the wording.
- inferred: one or two observations, or the Architect's derivation. Never
  acted on as a ruling.

A superseded entry stays, struck with `SUPERSEDED <date>: <new entry>`.
Scope: business and process only. Project facts go to project palaces; keep
no personal fact beyond what helps the work.
