```

  -> is a backup of palace live-9282, not other; nothing was written

  -> the manifest names palace "../evil", which is not a valid palace id; nothing was written
```

## Divergences between the guide and what was observed

1. **`trusty-memory stop` is unsafe in a sandbox.** The guide's restore step says
   `trusty-memory stop`, which stops every trusty-memory daemon for the user.
   The record used the sandbox script's `--stop` instead. The restore output
   also ends with `1. start the daemon: trusty-memory start`. The guide does not
   say these two commands act on every daemon. Not a defect in the commands.
2. **Manifest `writer_version` is `0.63.1`**, the trusty-common crate version,
   while the binary is `0.35.1`. The guide does not mention the field. A reader
   comparing it to `--version` will see two different numbers.
3. **Recall before re-embed is not empty.** The guide says recall finds the
   restored drawers only after `palace_reembed`. Observed: recall returned three
   drawers from layer 1 at a flat score of 0.075, with the matching drawer third.
   Findable, but unranked. The guide's statement holds for ranked (layer 2) recall.
4. **The first `dry_run=false` call reports pre-repair counts** (`missing 6`
   next to `repaired 6`). "Until it reports `missing: 0`" is correct but needs a
   second call even when the first repaired everything.
5. **Layer 2 score for the billing drawer moved from 0.688 to 0.538** after
   restore and re-embed, with identical text and ranking order. The other two
   scores are identical to 3 digits. Not explained by the record; the
   difference may come from state that is outside the backup (`recall.redb`,
   `last_used`).
6. **Refusal order.** With the daemon up, `--palace other` is refused as a
   mismatch, before the daemon-serving check. The guide lists the refusals
   without an order. With a non-empty target and a tampered backup, the
   non-empty check fires before the checksum check.
7. **Backup files in this run.** The guide lists `identity.txt`, deletion journals and
   `drawer_counts.jsonl` lines as backup contents. A fresh palace has none of
   these, so the backup held three files. Consistent with the guide, but those
   three paths are untested by this run.
