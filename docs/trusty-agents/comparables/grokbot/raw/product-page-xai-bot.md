# AI teammates that finish the work | Grok Bot

**Tagline:** AI teammates you can give real work to. Bots can sign in to your tools,
use them just like you do, and come back with finished work.

**Meta description:** Grok Bot is your team of always-on AI teammates. They have
their own computer, use it like you do, and never log off.

## Hero / positioning

- Meet Grok Bot
- Download for macOS / Contact sales
- Launch post link: "Grok Bot is here"

## Message Bots like teammates

Give tasks to Bots like you would a teammate on desktop or iOS. Your AI teammates
take projects from start to end, keep context on how you work and get smarter over
time, and come back when your approval is needed.

## Work with many Bots at once

Create a Bot, give it a task, and add another when the work grows—one on a project,
one on outbound, one on systems. AI teammates work in parallel, collaborate where
it makes sense, and keep working 24/7.

### Feature cards (page copy)

1. **Grok Bot works where you work** — Log Grok Bot in once. It uses your apps and
   websites just like you would, including the tools that are harder to navigate.
2. **Show a Bot how it's done** — Ask a Bot to follow along as you complete a
   workflow once. It saves it as a routine and runs it on its own next time.
3. **Bots get smarter over time** — Bots keep context and learn from each other.
   Show one a workflow today, hand off the project by Friday.
4. **Connect the Bots** — Put a few Bots in the same thread and they pass work
   between themselves. You watch them take action instead of approving every step.
