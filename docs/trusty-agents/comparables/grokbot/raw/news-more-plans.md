## Introducing Grok Bot (2026-08-11)

Grok Bot is your team of always-on agents. They have their own computer, work
inside tools and apps like you do, and keep working 24/7.

Launch positioning:

- Computer of its own (connectors/MCP + computer use)
- Message like a teammate (no workflow builder first)
- Many Bots at once / group coordination
- Show a Bot how it's done → routines
- Context compounds over time

**Initial beta access (launch day):** SuperGrok Heavy, Cursor Ultra, Cursor Teams
Premium on desktop and iOS (forum staff post). Enterprise waitlist.

Launch blog also listed SuperGrok / SuperGrok Plus / SuperGrok Heavy; Cursor Pro /
Pro+ / Ultra; Teams Standard and Premium as eligible in some copy — treat Aug 26
expansion as the clarification for Pro + all Teams.

Internal prototype examples: sales outbound, marketing, office ops, bug fixes;
chief-of-staff + specialist roster pattern.

## Grok Bot is now included with more plans (2026-08-26)

Expanding access. Included with:

- SuperGrok
- SuperGrok Plus
- SuperGrok Heavy
- Cursor Pro
- Cursor Pro+
- Cursor Ultra
- Cursor Teams Plans (Standard and Premium)

Grok Bot comes with its own usage, separate from Grok and Cursor plans.

Jobs listed in expansion post: sales prospector, website builder, digital
declutterer, customer support, game artist, office manager, inbox manager,
meeting stand-in, refunds manager.

Enterprise still waitlist at that date.

Staff confirmation (forum, Colin, 2026-08-28): access live for all paid individual
plans (Pro/Pro+/Ultra) and self-serve Teams; not percentage/region rollout.
Not available for legacy request-based pricing without opting into usage-based pricing.
Enterprise rollout continuing.

## Grok Bot for Enterprise (2026-09-03)

Grok Bot now available for enterprises. Grok and Cursor Enterprise customers:
free usage for next two weeks; can invite whole org including people without an
existing seat.

Adds access, network, and audit controls for governance at scale.

Named customer examples in post: Legora, Supermicro, ServiceTitan. Heaviest use
outside engineering (sales, recruiting, marketing, finance, engineering).

Secure by default: per-user isolated environment; no access by default.

## Plans and billing (cursor.com/help/grok-bot/plans)

- Included on every paid individual Cursor plan and Cursor Teams.
- Can also grant usage by linking individual SuperGrok / SuperGrok Plus /
  SuperGrok Heavy / X Premium+.
- SuperGrok Team or SuperGrok Enterprise linking not supported.
- SuperGrok Lite: not included.
- Weekly included usage; spillover to shared on-demand if enabled.
- Usage metered on Cursor account, not on Grok/X.
- SuperGrok/X Premium+ link is permanent once created (cannot unlink/move).
- Pro < Pro+ < Ultra weekly usage tiers.
- Self-serve Teams: every member has access; no Premium seat required.
- Enterprise: contact AE to enable.
