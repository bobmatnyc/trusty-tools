Presenter controls

Right / Space: reveal the next step, then advance
Left: previous slide
Shift + Right: skip to the next slide
N: speaker notes and sources
F: fullscreen
A: pause / resume moving packets
B: black screen
Home / End: first / last slide

Choose a slide from the menu. “Live example” opens the Duetto board or Console when relevant. All visuals and fonts are embedded for offline use.
