12 / PHILOSOPHY & GUARDRAILS

Foundry is a workshop, not a showroom. Extend it by following the rules below — every one is checkable in review.

PRINCIPLES
Machines talk mono. Anything a computer produced or consumes — ids, paths, counts, versions, labels — is IBM Plex Mono. Human prose is Plex Sans. Chakra Petch is reserved for headings.
Rust is the signal, paper is the field. #B7410E marks the one thing that matters on a surface: the primary action, the highlighted datum, the active nav. If two things are rust, neither is.
Flat and honest. Depth comes from clean 1.5px line borders and the raised #EFE6D8 header strip — never drop shadows on resting surfaces. Shadows are reserved for things that float: modals, toasts, menus.
The robot is a colleague. UNIT marks appear at brand moments (sidebar, empty states, chat avatar) — not as decoration on every card.
GUARDRAILS
DOUse only tokens from tokens.css; derive new tints in oklch by shifting lightness, keeping the rust hue (~40°).
DOGive every button tier a distinct background; pair status color with a text label or icon, never color alone.
DOKeep radii at 3/5/8px, borders at 1px (dividers) or 1.5px (containers), spacing on the 4px grid.
NOPills, gradients, curved charts, emoji, or offset "sticker" shadows.
NONew accent hues. Success/warning/danger/info are fixed; everything else stays in the rust-to-paper ramp.
NORust on more than one element per view region, and never as body text or backgrounds of large areas.
NONew fonts or weights. Three faces, weights 400–700, nothing below 10px mono / 12px sans.
